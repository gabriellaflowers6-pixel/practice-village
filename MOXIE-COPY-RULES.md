# Moxie Studios — copy rules

For anyone writing words that a Villager will read inside Moxie Studios: JoYi, Gabby, or a bot working for either of us. Assembled 2026-08-17 from the approved landing copy v4, JoYi's Village voice rules, and her rulings since.

Gabby owns edits to this file going forward.

---

## The voice

Plain, direct, kind, practical. A knowledgeable teacher talking to a grown woman, not a brand talking at a prospect.

- Full sentences that a person would say out loud. Read every line aloud before it ships.
- Second person. You, not "users" or "she" when the reader is the one being addressed.
- No em dashes. Commas, periods, and full stops do the work.
- Nuance over absolutes. "Most fitness apps," never "every fitness app." We work with nuance; sweeping claims about the world are out.
- Never rigid. Where a reader could arrive two ways, give two doors, not one prescription.

## Never say

| Banned | Why | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| **from zero** | Reads like she is nothing yet. Ruled out by JoYi 2026-08-17. | the basics, beginner, start with the basics |
| **Learn it properly** | Lectures her. Implies she has been doing it wrong. | Nothing. Describe the practice, not her failure. |
| girl | She is a woman. | woman |
| YMCA | It was the YWCA. | YWCA |
| journey, hold space, unlock, sacred, queen, bestie | Wellness costume. | say the plain thing |
| any demo or trial language inside the membership | She already paid for this room. | Begin your practice |
| pricing, seat counters, founding offers inside the membership | Same reason. Sales copy belongs on the public page only. | Included with your membership |
| clipped headline fragments as "punchy" copy | Reads like ad copy, not speech. | a sentence |

## Naming

- **Moxie** is the guide. Not Bott Om. That name was retired 2026-08-16; the logo mark is Zen Bott Om and stays where it is.
- **Moxie Studios**, plural. It is a room inside Practice Village, never a separate product with its own membership.
- **The mirror** is the form check. Not "the AI," not "pose detection."
- **Practice Village** is the whole. **The Village** is fine in running copy.
- **Your lobby** is where a member returns to, at `/member`.

## Claims we make, exactly

These are promises. They are not decoration and they do not get softened or embellished.

- The mirror runs on her own device. Video never leaves her machine.
- Nothing is recorded. Nobody is watching.
- The camera is optional every day. The whole practice works with it off.
- Every pose and lesson was reviewed with a human teacher before it reached her.
- **Live classes open February 7, 2027.** Not February 6, not "Lunar New Year." One date everywhere, and it matches every promise the Village has made.
- Live classes are included with membership. Camera and microphone are always her choice, and classes are never recorded.

## Claims we never make

- Medical, therapeutic, or diagnostic claims. We describe the practice, not outcomes we cannot promise.
- That a class, room, or feature is open before it is. Honest status labels beat optimistic ones.
- That the studio can see, watch, judge, or score her. The mirror shows her what her body is doing so she can check her own form.

## Who she is

A woman learning the basics at home. She has not found a studio yet, or she has been in one and felt intimidated or irritated, and she still knows this matters. Write for her, not for someone who already knows the poses.

## Inside the membership vs the public page

The public Village landing page sells. Everything under `/studio` is inside the membership, where she has already bought the room:

- No pricing, no seat counters, no founding offer, no Stripe links
- No "create account," no "sign in," no magic links. Practice Village signs her in once
- No demo or free-trial framing
- The way out is "Back to your lobby," never a marketing site

## When copy and code disagree

The live site is the source of truth for names, dates, and prices. If a doc says something the site does not, the doc is stale. Fix the doc.
