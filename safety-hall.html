Device safety

    

      
Text, recordings, and uploaded files stay in this browser. They are not sent to Practice Village, but anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to find them.

      
Quick exit replaces this page. It does not erase browser history, downloads, device backups, or activity visible to an administrator.

      
A personal device is safest. Avoid a work or shared device when documenting sensitive experiences.

      
Microphone access is reused only while this page stays open. You can release it at any time. Safety Hall also releases it when you use discreet view, quick exit, or leave the page.

      
Screenshots are kept as their original local files. Safety Hall does not read them, extract their text, or analyze their contents.

      
Exports contain authored text, editable transcripts, screenshot context, and metadata only. Download original recordings or files separately from a saved entry. Redacted screenshots are created only when you choose and downloaded as separate PNG copies.
