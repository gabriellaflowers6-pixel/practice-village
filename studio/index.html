Who this is for

Start here if this sounds like your year.

Your sleep, joints, and energy changed with perimenopause, and most fitness apps still train you like you're 25. The layoff took the job, the routine, and the gym budget in the same week. You haven't taken a class of any kind in years, and leaving the house for one feels like a lot. Or you tried a studio and spent the whole hour watching yourself be watched.

What the practice does

Yoga and meditation, one practice. Here is what it gives you.

Physical

Strength, balance, and steadier sleep. Weight-bearing poses support your bones, and that matters now: women lose up to 20 percent of bone density in the five to seven years after menopause. Breath-led core work supports the pelvic floor, which changes for about half of us. Every pose is layered so your joints set the pace.

Emotional

A calmer baseline. Fifteen minutes of practice is a working tool for a stressful day, and knowing a pose beats copying one: confidence comes from understanding what your body is doing and why.

Social

A community center of women, open now. Live classes open in February, and you walk in already knowing the practice. Isolation is a health risk on its own; this is a room out of the house that starts at home.

The studies on yoga, mindfulness, menopausal sleep and mood are public and linked in the footer. The faster proof is fifteen minutes on the mat tonight.

Meet Moxie

Your teacher is modeled on a real one.

Moxie is the woman on our logo, built from JoYi's own movement and recorded in JoYi's own voice. She gets into every position with you: sitting, standing, lying down, holding. She teaches each pose from the ground up, feet first, because the base creates the action and the action creates the shape. When your attention wanders, she says what a teacher in the room would say: wandering is normal, returning is the practice.

"Unclench and sit with us. You can let this be simple."
Two ways to begin

Start at zero, or start where you are.

Beginner means different things. Some of us know absolutely nothing and want to be taught. Some of us know some poses, half-remember a class from years ago, and want easy practice without a curriculum in the way. The studio holds both doors open, and you can walk between them any day.

Door one

The First 30 Days

Brand new? One pose a day, 10 to 15 minutes a session, always the same shape: arrive, revisit yesterday's pose from memory, learn one new pose in layers, hold it with the mirror watching if you invite it, one steady breath to close. Week one is ground and breath. Week two is the floor. Week three is standing poses. Week four links what you know into a full 20-minute Hatha sequence. Know a pose already? Show the mirror and the course moves at your speed.

Door two

Simple Sequences

Been on a mat before? Guided beginner sequences are open from day one: a gentle morning stretch, an easy standing flow, a rest practice, the evening wind-down, and short sits from one minute to twenty. The wind-down includes two body-scan recordings, one for you alone and one to play with kids in the room. Moxie checks in, points you toward what fits today, and gets out of your way.

Show up, it counts. Days advance when you show up. Miss four days and day 12 waits for you, exactly where you left it.

The easier version is the real version. Knees down, blocks, wall: all of it counts as the pose.

Your record grows. Every practice ends in your record: the growing list of poses you know, yours to keep, yours to export.

Live classes

Sunday, February 7, 2027 · sunrise and sunset · Central Time

JoYi finishes her 200-hour teacher training in October. In February, Moxie Studios opens its live room with sunrise and sunset classes: yoga, meditation, and stretch.

Live classes are a separate, optional room. You choose whether to turn on your camera or microphone before entering. Other people see or hear you only when you choose to participate that way. Classes are never recorded, and the mirror still runs privately on your own machine.

Live classes are included with your membership. Graduates of the First 30 Days are seated first.

Questions

Asked and answered.

Do I need to be flexible, thin, young, or experienced?

You need a floor and 15 minutes. The studio starts with the basics, the sequences meet you where you are, and every pose has an easier version that counts.

What if I hate being on camera?

The camera is optional every single day. Every practice works with it off; the mirror is there for the days you want a teacher's eye on your form.

Who is teaching me?

JoYi Rhyss: meditation teacher, Attitudinal Healing facilitator, 200-hour yoga teacher training complete in October. Moxie moves and speaks from JoYi's own practice, and February's live classes are taught by JoYi, live. The studio is built the way real studios are: room for more teachers as it grows.

What exactly is in the membership today?

The wind-down recordings, the meditation room as sessions ship this fall, the Simple Sequences as they ship, the First 30 Days first cohort, the open Village rooms, and founding early entry in February. The room grid above is the current state of everything.

What happens to my data?

Camera video is processed on your device and never uploaded. Your record saves what you choose, and you can read, export, or delete it any time.

Refunds?

Thirty days, full refund, no questions. Founding seats that refund go back to the pool of 108.
