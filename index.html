good questions

Before you walk in

Is the whole place built yet?

No. We open one room at a time. Open today: Moxie Studio, with beginner yoga and meditation guided by Bott Om and the mirror to support your form; the Kitchen (PlantLuck); and the free Quiet Room (HUSH). Safety Hall is in build; Your PIL is rolling out. Live classes with JoYi open February 2027. Founding members enter first and help shape each room.

Is this therapy or coaching?

Neither. Practice Village is a practice environment. It does not diagnose or treat, and it never replaces a therapist, doctor, attorney, or crisis service. When something needs a professional, the Concierge points you to vetted resources.

What is the AI actually doing?

The Concierge runs on Google's Gemini. It answers your question, gives you one clear next step, and suggests the room that can help. It saves to Your PIL only when you choose. In Moxie Studio, the mirror reads your form on your device and never records video. People teach the classes.

How is my information handled?

Nothing sensitive is saved without your yes. What you choose to save lives in Your PIL, where you can read, export, or delete it at any time. Camera video in Moxie Studio is processed on your device and never uploaded. We never sell or share your information.

Who is behind it?

JoYi Rhyss, who led this work in person at Moxie Fitness and The Practice Center in Hilo, Hawaii. Practice Village brings the same practice online for women everywhere.
