this is not theory

I've been practicing.

I'm JoYi. For years, I worked with women through Moxie Fitness and The Practice Center in Hilo, Hawaiʻi. We moved our bodies, sat in hard conversations, practiced mindfulness and forgiveness, made clearer choices, and helped each other through change.

That work happened in real rooms, with real people, over time.

Practice Village grows directly from it. We are taking the practices, support, movement, teaching, and community that helped women in those rooms and building ways for more women to access them wherever they are.

Moxie Fitness began in Hawaiʻi. Practice Village opens the door wider.

good questions

Before you walk in

Is the whole place built yet?

No. We open one room at a time. Open today: Moxie Studios, with beginner yoga and meditation guided by Moxie and the mirror to support your form; the Kitchen (PlantLuck); and the free sixty-second HUSH app. The HUSH room holds a vetted shelf of mindfulness resources, and the Kitchen holds one for plant-forward eating and finding food near you. Safety Hall is open, free for everyone; Your Record is open with membership. Live classes with JoYi open February 7, 2027. Founding members enter first and help shape each room.

Is this therapy or coaching?

Neither. Practice Village is a practice environment. It does not diagnose or treat, and it never replaces a therapist, doctor, attorney, or crisis service. When something needs a professional, the Concierge points you to vetted resources.

What is the AI actually doing?

The Concierge runs on Google's Gemini. It answers your question, gives you one clear next step, and suggests the room that can help. It saves to your Record only when you choose. In Moxie Studios, the mirror reads your form on your device and never records video. People teach the classes.

How is my information handled?

Nothing sensitive is saved without your yes. What you choose to save lives in your Record, where you can read, export, or delete it at any time. Camera video in Moxie Studios is processed on your device and never uploaded. We never sell or share your information.

Who is behind it?

JoYi Rhyss, who led this work in person at Moxie Fitness and The Practice Center in Hilo, Hawaii. Practice Village brings the same practice online for women everywhere. Meet the builders →
