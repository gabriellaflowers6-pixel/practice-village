this is not theory

I have already built this in real rooms.

I'm JoYi. For years, I worked with women at Moxie Fitness and The Practice Center in Hilo, Hawaiʻi, helping them rebuild through movement, practice, honest conversation, clear choices, and community.

Practice Village grows from that work. It takes what happened in those rooms, the courage, the practice, the support, the next step, and brings it online for women navigating major life changes everywhere.

Moxie Fitness began in Hawai'i. Practice Village opens the door wider.

the builders

We're building in the open.

We are building Practice Village because human potential should not be buried under red tape, dead ends, broken systems, or support that hands people information but no real way through. Especially for women rebuilding their lives.

Practice Village begins with the Concierge: an AI-supported guide that helps clear the noise, name the next step, and move through systems that too often leave people stuck.

But the real work is not only logistical. It is human. It is the clear choice. The practice. The support. The courage to begin again. The next best action. We help with that too.

The three of us are building Practice Village in the open, room by room, as our entry in the Build with Gemini XPRIZE for Education & Human Potential.

As the rooms open, step inside with us. Experience what we are building. Tell us what is missing. Tell us what would have helped you sooner.

If you are rebuilding any part of your life and want real people in it with you, come into the Village.

good questions

Before you walk in

Is the whole place built yet?

No, and we're not pretending otherwise. We're opening Practice Village room by room, in the open. You can join and reserve your founding spot today. Safety Hall and the Resource Library open June 29. The Founding Circle starts July 11. Your record and the WhatsApp porch follow within weeks, then the Commons (your social fitness room) and Moxie Studio. Founding members get in first and help shape each room.

Is this therapy or coaching?

Neither. It's a practice environment. It doesn't diagnose or treat, and it never replaces a therapist, doctor, attorney, or crisis service. It points you to vetted resources when something's outside its lane.

What's the AI actually doing?

The Concierge does real work: it cuts through the bureaucracy and noise, turns it into one clear next step, builds your record, and (as the Commons opens) matches you to the right people. A live human guide and you do the authentically-human part.

How is my information handled?

Consent-led, always. Nothing sensitive is saved without your yes, private stays private, and you can export or delete your record anytime.

Who's behind it?

JoYi, who has guided this work in real life at Moxie Fitness and The Practice Center in Hilo, Hawaii. Practice Village makes it AI-powered and brings it to women everywhere.
