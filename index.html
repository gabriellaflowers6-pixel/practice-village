good questions

Before you walk in

Is the whole place built yet?

No, and we're not pretending otherwise. We're opening Practice Village room by room, in the open. Open today: Moxie Studio, the Kitchen (PlantLuck), and the free Quiet Room (HUSH). Safety Hall is in build and the Record Room is rolling out. Live classes with JoYi open February 2027. Founding members get in first and help shape each room.

Is this therapy or coaching?

Neither. It's a practice environment. It doesn't diagnose or treat, and it never replaces a therapist, doctor, attorney, or crisis service. It points you to vetted resources when something's outside its lane.

What's the AI actually doing?

The Concierge does real work: it cuts through the bureaucracy and noise, turns it into one clear next step, and builds your record. In Moxie Studio, the mirror reads your form on your own device and never records you. A live human guide and you do the authentically-human part.

How is my information handled?

Consent-led, always. Nothing sensitive is saved without your yes, private stays private, and you can export or delete your record anytime.

Who's behind it?

JoYi, who has guided this work in real life at Moxie Fitness and The Practice Center in Hilo, Hawaii. Practice Village makes it AI-powered and brings it to women everywhere.
