Meet the builders.

Human potential should not be buried under red tape, dead ends, or support that gives people information without helping them figure out what to do with it.

Practice Village is being built for women rebuilding parts of their lives who need practical help finding their way forward.

The Concierge

The Concierge starts with what you are trying to figure out. It finds the official information that applies to you, explains it in plain language, and helps you work through what to do next. It can also bring back practices, lessons, and choices that helped you before, so when something familiar comes up again, you are not starting from scratch. More about the Concierge →

Moxie Studios

Moxie is for people who want to try yoga or meditation but are not ready for a classroom, are nervous about being watched or corrected, or have followed a video and wondered whether they were doing the poses correctly. It gives beginners a private place to learn, repeat, and build confidence. Every pose and lesson is developed and reviewed with a human teacher before it reaches you.

How we build

Practice Village is agent-first. AI works throughout the Village to find information, connect what you have already learned, and help you take action instead of handing you another page of resources.

We are builders, not traditional software developers, building Practice Village with Gemini, Claude, and Codex. The entire digital community center is our entry in the Build with Gemini XPRIZE for Education & Human Potential.

Rooms are opening now, and we will continue building through the rest of the year. Live classes begin in Moxie Studios on February 7, 2027.
